Patient Parameter Reference Guide

Detailed documentation of the 13 input parameters used for machine learning inference

13 Key Features

1. Age (Patient Age)

Demographic

Represents patient age in years. Cardiac workloads, vessel elasticity, and arterial plaque calcification naturally trend higher with age. Acceptable dataset range is between 29.0 and 77.0 years.

Clinical Range: 29 - 77 years

2. Sex (Patient Gender)

Demographic

Binary indicator mapping gender of the patient: Male (1) or Female (0). Estrogen hormone profiles generally offer cardiovascular protection in younger females.

Allowed Values: 1 = Male, 0 = Female

3. cp (Chest Pain Type)

Symptomatic

Indicates chest pain characteristics. Value classification maps as follows:

  • 0 - Typical Angina: Severe chest pain resulting from reduced cardiac blood supply.
  • 1 - Atypical Angina: Non-classical chest pain, sometimes presenting in females or diabetic patients.
  • 2 - Non-anginal Pain: Non-cardiac chest discomfort (e.g. esophageal or muscle spasm).
  • 3 - Asymptomatic: Absence of chest pain (note: can mask underlying ischemic conditions).

4. trestbps (Resting Blood Pressure)

Vital Indicator

Resting systolic blood pressure in mmHg recorded upon hospital admission. Acceptable range is 94 to 200 mmHg. Supports quick-slash inputs such as 120/80 (automatically extracts systolic pressure).

Normal Range: < 120 mmHg Hypertension: ≥ 130 mmHg

5. chol (Serum Cholesterol)

Vital Indicator

Serum cholesterol levels in mg/dl (Range: 126–564 mg/dl). Desirable range is below 200 mg/dL. Elevated cholesterol correlates with atherosclerotic plaque buildup.

Desirable: < 200 mg/dL High Risk: ≥ 240 mg/dL

6. fbs (Fasting Blood Sugar)

Vital Indicator

Fasting blood sugar indicator: 1 = True (> 120 mg/dl); 0 = False. Blood sugar elevations raise risk of diabetic microvascular complications.

Allowed Values: 1 = Elevated (>120 mg/dL), 0 = Normal

7. restecg (Resting ECG Results)

Cardiac Test

Resting electrocardiographic results:

  • 0 - Normal: Waveforms and rhythm are physiologically normal.
  • 1 - ST-T Wave Abnormality: Minor variations suggesting ischemic stress or electrolyte imbalances.
  • 2 - Left Ventricular Hypertrophy: Thickening of the left ventricle wall (common in chronic hypertension).

8. thalach (Max Heart Rate Achieved)

Vital Indicator

Maximum heart rate in bpm achieved during a clinical stress test. Range: 71 to 202 bpm. Lower maximums often suggest impaired cardiovascular output.

Common Range: 71 - 202 bpm

9. exang (Exercise Angina)

Symptomatic

Exercise induced angina indicator: 1 = Yes; 0 = No. A positive reading indicates arterial restriction under heavy cardiovascular load.

Allowed Values: 1 = Present, 0 = Absent

10. oldpeak (ST Depression)

Cardiac Test

ST segment depression induced by physical exercise relative to rest (Min: 0.0, Max: 6.2). Values greater than 1.0 are clinically significant, suggesting ischemic myocardium.

Normal Range: 0.0 - 1.0 Ischemic Danger: > 1.0

11. slope (ST Segment Slope)

Cardiac Test

Slope of the peak exercise ST segment:

  • 0 - Upsloping: Common in normal exercise physiology.
  • 1 - Flat: Indication of possible border-line cardiac strain.
  • 2 - Downsloping: Frequently correlates with coronary artery lesions.

12. ca (Number of Major Vessels)

Cardiac Test

Number of major vessels (0-3) colored during fluoroscopy scan. High values represent calcification, indicating blood flow blockage.

Desirable: 0 vessels colored Occlusion Indicators: 1 - 3 vessels colored

13. thal (Thalassemia Defect)

Cardiac Test

Reflects blood flow perfusion scan findings:

  • 1 - Fixed Defect: Irreversible coronary tissue damage (often post-infarct).
  • 2 - Normal: Blood flow is normal and uninterrupted.
  • 3 - Reversible Defect: Blood flow restriction only visible under physical load.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the heart disease prediction model work?

Our platform utilizes a machine learning classifier (Random Forest/Logistic Regression) trained on clinical observations (including the Cleveland Heart Disease Dataset). It evaluates the non-linear relationship between 13 patient indicators simultaneously to calculate an aggregate risk percentage and class indicator.

What do the predicted risk scores represent?

  • LOW RISK (< 40% Probability): Key biomarkers align with healthy zones. Standard preventative checkups are recommended.
  • MEDIUM RISK (40% - 69% Probability): Multiple indicators lie outside optimal bounds. Schedule a doctor consultation.
  • HIGH RISK (≥ 70% Probability): Heavy correlation with ischemic history and blockages. Immediate clinical attention is highly advised.

Can the system automatically parse clinical reports?

Yes! The Dashboard supports drag-and-drop file upload for PDF reports or medical laboratory sheets. The system uses a local regex parsing fallback alongside advanced Gemini Vision to extract age, cholesterol, BP, ECG findings, and more, automatically prepopulating the patient submission form.

How do I access and manage PDF health report records?

Go to the "Reports" page in the sidebar. You can review past predictions, download comprehensive PDF diagnostic sheets (comprising charts, interpretations, and user parameters), or delete specific reports from history.
