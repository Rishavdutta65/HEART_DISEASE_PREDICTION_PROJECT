System Guide & Documentation

What This System Does

The Heart Disease Prediction System leverages advanced machine learning models to predict a patient's likelihood of detecting heart disease. By inputting 13 key medical attributes, the engine instantly computes a risk score and provides intelligent insights, allowing for quicker and more precise health analysis.

How To Use The Predictor

1

Enter Details

Navigate to the Patient Input tab and fill all the medical attributes accurately.

2

Click Predict

Hit the Predict Now button to send data securely to our AI model.

3

Review Results

Examine the risk score, feature importance charts, and medical recommendations.

4

Generate Report

Download a detailed PDF report containing the prediction results and insights.

Explanation of Medical Fields

Click a card to focus and read details

Age

Age in Years
Demographics
Simple Meaning

Your age in years.

Valid Range 29–77 years
Example 45
Why it Matters

Heart disease risk generally increases with age as blood vessels naturally stiffen and plaque builds up.

Sex

Biological Sex
Demographics
Options

Male = 1, Female = 0.

Valid Range 0 or 1
Example 1 (Male)
Why it Matters

Men generally show higher incidence of cardiovascular events at an earlier age, while women's risk escalates post-menopause.

Chest Pain Type

cp
Symptoms
Options
0: Typical Angina 1: Atypical Angina 2: Non-anginal Pain 3: Asymptomatic
Valid Range 0 to 3
Example 0
Why it Matters

Angina is key warning sign of blocked arteries. Typical angina (0) strongly suggests heart artery obstruction.

Resting BP

trestbps
Vitals
Simple Meaning

Blood pressure while resting.

Valid Range 94–200 mmHg
Example 120
Why it Matters

Chronic high blood pressure forces the heart to work harder, causing the heart muscle to thicken and weaken over time.

Cholesterol

chol
Vitals
Simple Meaning

Serum cholesterol level.

Valid Range 126–564 mg/dL
Example 200
Why it Matters

High levels of serum cholesterol contribute directly to atherosclerosis (hardening of the arteries) by forming fatty plaques.

Fasting Sugar

fbs
Vitals
Options

1: Sugar > 120 mg/dL (True)
0: Sugar ≤ 120 mg/dL (False)

Valid Range 0 or 1
Example 0
Why it Matters

Elevated fasting blood sugar is associated with diabetes, which accelerates artery damage and double/triples heart disease risk.

Resting ECG

restecg
ECG/Stress
Options
0: Normal 1: ST-T wave abnormality 2: Left ventricular hypertrophy
Valid Range 0 to 2
Example 0
Why it Matters

ECG details reveal underlying cardiac abnormalities like ventricular strain or history of silent myocardial infarction.

Max Heart Rate

thalach
ECG/Stress
Simple Meaning

Highest heart rate during stress test.

Valid Range 71–202 bpm
Example 150
Why it Matters

Abnormal maximum heart rate response during exercise can indicate weakness or cardiovascular limitation.

Exercise Angina

exang
Symptoms
Options

1: Angina triggered by exercise (Yes)
0: No angina triggered (No)

Valid Range 0 or 1
Example 0
Why it Matters

Chest pain triggered specifically by physical exertion indicates that coronary arteries cannot supply enough blood under stress.

ST Depression

oldpeak
ECG/Stress
Simple Meaning

Heart stress measurement during exercise.

Valid Range 0.0–6.2
Example 1.5
Why it Matters

Depression of ST segment on ECG during stress indicates ischemia (insufficient blood flow to the heart muscle).

ST Segment Slope

slope
ECG/Stress
Options

0: Upsloping, 1: Flat, 2: Downsloping.

Valid Range 0 to 2
Example 1
Why it Matters

Flat or downsloping ST segments are strong indicators of ischemia or severe coronary artery disease.

Major Vessels

ca
Diagnostics
Simple Meaning

Number of major blood vessels colored by fluoroscopy.

Valid Range 0 to 3 vessels
Example 0
Why it Matters

Fluoroscopy highlights blood flow. A higher number of uncolored vessels (0 to 3) suggests more blood flow blockage.

Thalassemia

thal
Diagnostics
Options

1 = Fixed Defect, 2 = Normal, 3 = Reversible Defect.

Valid Range 1 to 3
Example 2 (Normal)
Why it Matters

A reversible defect means blood flow is reduced during stress but normal at rest, showing temporary ischemia.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool a replacement for a doctor?

No. This tool provides predictive insights based on machine learning, but should never replace professional medical diagnosis.

Is my medical data stored?

Predictions are processed instantly. If you use the chat or report features, minimal session data is temporarily kept to generate your PDFs but is cleared systematically.
